Overview

Leaderboard

Mean over the selected songs and difficulties. Every method is scored by the same code against the real maps. Hover a bar for the spread.

All metrics at a glance

The same means for every metric. Timing, colour, placement and cut: higher is better. Density: 1.0 means as many notes as the real map. Swing errors: lower is better (the real maps' own rate is the reference line).

Song by song

The selected metric for every song and method (difficulties averaged unless one is selected). Click a song to inspect it below.

Timing

How far each generated note is from the nearest real note. A score counts a note as on time within 30 ms (the shaded band). A method whose whole map is early or late shows a shifted peak and a best shift away from zero.

Onset snapping

Moving each generated note to the nearest onset in the audio, if one is within the window. A clean-up step, not the model: it shows how much of the timing gap is precision rather than choosing the wrong moments.

One song up close

Virtual player

Two players swing the selected song side by side: the real map on the left (an expert's motion), another map on the right. Each cut is a stroke through its note in its arrow's direction; between cuts the saber curves from one cut into the next, and the arms follow by inverse kinematics from the shoulders. Good flow is a smooth pendulum. Flagged: a hand having to reposition between cuts faster than 99% of this song's real map asks (a snap), and the arms crossing. Click a flagged moment to jump there.

Training

Held-out loss (on BeatSaver songs each run never trained on) over training time. Lower is better. Runs with different data sizes hold out different songs, so compare shapes more than exact values.
